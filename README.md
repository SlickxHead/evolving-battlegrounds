# Evolving Battlegrounds

A fantasy auto-battler where your creatures evolve. Recruit, combine triples, evolve, and outlast 7 rivals.

**Play:** https://slickxhead.github.io/evolving-battlegrounds/

On your phone, open the link and add it to your home screen: on iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari; on Android, tap ⋮ → Install app in Chrome. It runs full screen and works offline.
